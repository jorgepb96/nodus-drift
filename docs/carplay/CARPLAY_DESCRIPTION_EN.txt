Nodus Drift provides local playback of layered environmental soundscapes and
instrumental ambient audio. People can prepare and save mixes and evolving audio
scenes on iPhone, then select and listen to them through CarPlay.

The CarPlay interface uses the CarPlay framework's audio templates. Home provides
Continue Listening and recent selections. My Mixes displays saved mixes and
Studio scenes using native static artwork grids. Explore provides a small set of
ready-to-play soundscapes. Selecting an item starts local audio playback and opens
the native Now Playing screen, with the soundscape title and static cover art.
Playback supports system and vehicle play/pause controls and respects audio
interruptions. Browsing and playback do not require network access or interaction
with the connected iPhone.

CarPlay does not expose scene editing, timelines, AI prompts, audio generation,
model downloads, account setup, visual animations, meditation exercises or sleep
guidance. These remain outside the vehicle interface. The CarPlay experience is
focused on selecting and playing existing audio content with minimal interaction.
