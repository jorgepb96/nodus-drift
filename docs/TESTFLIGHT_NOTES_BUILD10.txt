Photorealistic immersive landscapes

This private build replaces the flat illustrated scenery with seven detailed photographic-style environments: forest, coast, mountains, cabin, river, snow and night.

What to test:
- Open a scene in Soundscape Studio, choose Visuals, then enter the full-screen landscape and hide the controls.
- Look for subtle water ripples, moving foliage, layered rain and snow, drifting mist, fireplace flames and warm flickering light, and stars in the night sky.
- Adjust rain, wind, daylight, fog and fire. Weather follows the scene's layer intensity and timeline automation; cabin rain stays outside the window.
- Pause and resume the scene. Animation should stop when paused, offscreen or in the background, and with Reduce Motion, Low Power Mode or serious thermal pressure.
- Check the portrait view and larger iPad layouts, plus the option to turn visuals off completely.

All landscape artwork is included and works offline. No image-generation account or download is required. The existing catalogue, simple mixer, saved mixes, timer, Nodi, scene editing, spatial audio and optional local audio-generation tools remain available.

Please report visual artifacts, unexpected motion, excessive warmth, or performance issues together with your device model. This build was checked in Simulator; no new physical-device tests were performed.
