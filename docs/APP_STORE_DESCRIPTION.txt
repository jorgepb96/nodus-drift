Make a little space to be.

Nodus Drift brings 89 ambient sounds and Nodi, your quiet animated companion, to iPhone and iPad. Find rain, waves, birds and everyday places, plus white, pink and brown noise and stereo binaural tones. Every sound works offline.

Blend up to six sounds, adjust each layer and the master volume, mark your favorites, and save the mixes you want to return to. Search and sort the library to find the right sound for your moment.

Set a sleep timer and keep your soundscape playing with the screen locked. Drift into a dark, full-screen meditation scene with Nodi. The controls fade away while Nodi rests; tap anywhere to open Nodi's eyes and bring them back.

Designed for iPhone and iPad, with support for VoiceOver, larger text and Reduce Motion. Use stereo headphones for binaural tones.

Free. No account, ads, tracking, subscriptions or purchases. Available in English.
