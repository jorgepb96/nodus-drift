Make a little space to be.

Nodus Drift brings 89 ambient sounds and Nodi, your quiet animated companion, to iPhone and iPad. Ideal for reading and studying, it lets you create a quiet backdrop for your time with a book or your notes. Find rain, waves, birds and everyday places, plus white, pink and brown noise and stereo binaural tones. Every sound works offline.

Blend up to six sounds, adjust each layer and the master volume, mark your favorites, and save the mixes you want to return to. Search and sort the library to find the right sound for your moment.

Set a sleep timer and keep your soundscape playing with the screen locked. Drift into a dark, full-screen meditation scene with Nodi.

Free. No account, ads, tracking, subscriptions or purchases. Available in English.
