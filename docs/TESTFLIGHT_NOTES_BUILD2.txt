Welcome to Nodus Drift 0.1.0 (2). The app is in English and all 89 sounds work offline.

Please test these experiences:

SOUNDS AND MIXES
Browse, search and sort the library. Mark favorites, combine up to six sound layers, and adjust individual and master volume. Save a named mix, then load it again; saved mixes should load paused.

A QUIET MOMENT WITH NODI
Open the full-screen meditation scene. After five seconds the controls fade away, leaving Nodi resting on a dark background. Tap anywhere to open Nodi's eyes and reveal the controls. Check entering, leaving and rotating the scene.

PLAYBACK ON YOUR DEVICE
Set a sleep timer and lock the screen. Check background playback, lock-screen play/pause and that audio stops when the timer ends. Try wired headphones, Bluetooth, interruptions and unplugging headphones. Use stereo headphones for binaural tones.

ACCESSIBILITY AND EVERYDAY USE
Try larger text, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion. Tell us about battery use, unexpected pauses, audio glitches or layout issues on your iPhone or iPad.

No account, network connection, payment, ads or subscription is required. Please send your feedback and screenshots through TestFlight.
