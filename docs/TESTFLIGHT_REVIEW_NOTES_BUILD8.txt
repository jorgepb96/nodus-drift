Nodus Drift is a free native iPhone and iPad ambient mixer. No login, account, companion app, network connection or purchases are needed. All 89 options work offline. Choose a starter mix in Sounds, then press Play in My mix; up to six voices may play together. Loading a saved mix stays paused. Selecting an individual sound starts playback. Use stereo headphones for the binaural tones.

Build 8 fixes a blank library after scrolling deeply and choosing Favorites. The first filtered results or empty-state explanation now appear below the pinned search and filters. Tab changes return to the top while retaining searches and categories. Clear empties the current mix immediately and cancels its sleep timer; saved mixes and favorites remain. The expand button opens dark meditation mode; tapping wakes Nodi and restores controls. Background audio is only for user-initiated playback. No medical effects are claimed.

The owner has licensed this iOS implementation under MIT. All 81 former Moodist recordings were replaced by newly produced loops: 80 options from 77 individually documented CC0 originals plus one original radio-tuning sound design without broadcast samples. Source creators, URLs and permission notices, Moodist catalogue MIT and FSD50K dataset acknowledgement are included in Settings → Moodist license. Eight noise/binaural generators run on device. Privacy policy: https://nodusresearch.com/drift/privacy/.
