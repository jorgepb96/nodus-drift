Nodus Drift 0.1.0 (7) — Navigation fixes and the first public beta.

WHAT CHANGED
• Sounds, My mix, Saved and Settings now open at the top whenever you switch tabs, even after scrolling deeply. Searches and filters stay selected.
• Clear in My mix immediately empties the active soundscape and cancels its sleep timer, without a confirmation dialog. Favorites and saved mixes remain.
• Nodus Drift for iOS now carries the owner's MIT license. Moodist catalogue, CC0 audio source and FSD50K notices remain available in Settings.

PLEASE CHECK
Scroll down in each tab, switch away and return: the heading should be visible again. Search or choose a category, switch away and return: your filter should remain. Create a mix, set a sleep timer, then tap Clear once. Restore a saved mix and confirm it remains paused and the old timer is gone.

The same 89 options remain, including 81 newly produced loops from the documented replacement bank and eight native noise/binaural generators. When ready to listen in a suitable place, check the sounds with headphones. Whale uses its CC0 author's vocal imitation; Wolf and Beehive are documented sound designs, and Carousel is amusement-park ambience. Please report repetitive loops or sounds that do not fit their label.

Free, offline and English-only in this beta, with no account, tracking, ads, purchases or subscriptions. Interaction and layout checks were silent; no auditory quality test was performed. Send feedback and screenshots through TestFlight.
