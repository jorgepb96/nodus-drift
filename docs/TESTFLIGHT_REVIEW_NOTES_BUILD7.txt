Public TestFlight beta of Nodus Drift, a native iPhone and iPad ambient mixer. No login, account, companion app, network connection or purchases are needed. All 89 options work offline. Choose a starter mix in Sounds, then press Play in My mix; up to six voices may play together. Loading a saved mix stays paused. Selecting an individual sound starts playback. Use stereo headphones for the binaural tones.

Scroll returns to the top when changing tabs, with searches and filters retained. Clear empties the current mix immediately and cancels its sleep timer; saved mixes and favorites remain. The expand button opens dark meditation mode. Controls hide after five seconds; tapping the screen opens Nodi's eyes and brings controls back. Reduce Motion is respected and VoiceOver keeps controls visible. Background audio is used only for user-initiated ambient playback. No medical effects are claimed.

The owner has licensed this iOS implementation under MIT. All 81 former Moodist recordings were replaced by newly produced loops: 80 options from 77 individually documented CC0 originals plus one original radio-tuning sound design without broadcast samples. Source creators, URLs and permission notices, Moodist catalogue MIT and FSD50K dataset acknowledgement are included in Settings → Moodist license. Eight noise/binaural generators run on device.
