Nodus Drift, Nodi and the connected N mark
This SwiftUI application adapts the Nodus Drift experience and Nodi's vector
character, lotus pose and original connected N logo from jorgepb96/nodus, revision
68fa0210dc8407938d644978291840d85aeb49b1.

The complete Nodus Drift MIT notice is included in Settings. This decision is specific to Drift for iOS; earlier Nodus releases retain their existing licenses. The development repository remains private. MIT does not require publishing corresponding source. Third-party material retains its own notices and permissions below.

The original Nodi vector geometry/animation and connected N SVG are also
available under the MIT license shipped with Nodus v3.2.7, commit
cfc0f7f03649fe4a6b80bd2ad97b4b7a81aa6441. Its Nodi.tsx, nodi.css and
nodus-logo.svg files are byte-identical to the current reference versions.
Their historical copyright and full MIT permission notice are retained in
NODUS_HISTORICAL_MIT.txt, accessible in Settings. The later September DRIFT lotus pose and first-party adaptation are covered separately by the owner’s MIT license decision for this iOS release.
The N's original path, four nodes and violet gradient are rendered natively.
The in-app mark follows Nodus Mobile's painted-bounds sizing; the app icon stays Nodi.

Moodist catalogue
The 81 recordings' identifiers, English labels and file paths originate from
remvze/moodist, revision 11c0be2200116a3635880d600fd6953899cc51a3.
Moodist's code and catalogue data are MIT licensed, © 2023 MAZE. The full MIT
notice is included in Settings. No Moodist application code is bundled.

Audio recordings
The original Moodist audio bank was entirely replaced in this release. Eighty
newly edited loop options derive from 77 individually documented CC0 1.0
originals. The radio loop is original Nodus synthesis without broadcast samples.
Authors, source URLs, license evidence and hashes are listed in AUDIO_SOURCES.md.
The CC0 legal text is available in Settings. Nodus owns the new editing and
sound-design contributions; the underlying public-domain permissions remain.
FSD50K's dataset compilation is CC BY 4.0; its curators, paper, license and
changes are acknowledged in AUDIO_SOURCES.md and FSD50K-LICENSE.txt.
Clotho supplies four individually CC0 audio files; its captions and code are
not used. No Moodist recording or untraced Pixabay recording is bundled.

Noise and binaural tones
White, pink and brown noise and the five binaural presets are generated on
your device by this app's native audio code. No third-party noise or binaural
recordings are used. Stereo headphones separate the binaural channels.
Sound descriptions state their physical properties and make no medical claims.
