Landscape artwork

The 22 portrait and 22 independently composed horizontal environment plates,
and two preserved transparent animal/object atlases, were
created for Nodus Drift using the OpenAI image generation tool in November 2026.
They are AI-generated photographic-style artwork, rather than documentary
photographs of identifiable locations. No external stock-photo collection is used.

All artwork is bundled with the app and works offline. The app does not call
an image-generation service, upload scene descriptions or download visual assets.
Native Metal shaders add water, foliage, precipitation, mist, firelight, stars,
footprints, glass droplets and other effects. Decorative animal/object overlays
are currently disabled; their artwork and scene data remain preserved for future use.

Nodus Drift's rendering code is distributed under the project's MIT license.
The artwork is provided with Nodus Drift; this notice does not claim exclusive
copyright in AI-generated imagery or alter the separate licenses of audio assets,
audio models or dependencies.

Prompts, file hashes and generation provenance are recorded in the source
repository at docs/soundscape-studio/LANDSCAPE_ASSETS.json and
docs/soundscape-studio/LANDSCAPE_WIDE_ASSETS.json.
