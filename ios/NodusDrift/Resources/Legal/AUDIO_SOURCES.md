Nodus Drift · Audio sources and permissions · October 9, 2026

All 81 file-based loops were newly produced for Nodus Drift. Eighty options use 77 individually documented CC0 1.0 source files; several options use different excerpts of the same source. The radio-tuning loop is original deterministic Nodus sound design without any third-party broadcast, voice or music samples. All 89 existing sound options remain available, including the eight on-device noise and binaural generators.

CC0 permits copying, modifying and distributing the original recordings, including in a free app. The original source dedication remains in effect. Nodus owns its new editing and sound-design contributions; they are distinguished from the underlying public-domain sources. No new dedication of Nodus-owned material is asserted here. The project owner instructed production and inclusion of this bank in the free app. Source recipes, original and output SHA-256 digests, per-file byte comparisons and evidence are preserved with the application source.

Sources were selected by matching the existing sound options and checking individual licenses. Where BigSoundBank supplies community ratings, its highest-rated relevant candidates were prioritized; ties also consider duration, suitability for looping and absence of music. FSD50K and Clotho metadata do not supply community quality scores. Their label-validation scores are not used as sound-quality ratings. This is not a claim of a global ranking of every recording online.

Processing: 44.1 kHz, original mono or stereo, high-pass filtering, circular source joins, deliberate pauses for discrete events, level balancing with soft compression and MP3 encoding. File sizes stay within 1% of the previous per-option budgets. iOS makes a 200 ms circular join at the playback boundary. No audio was played during production or technical validation. Auditory quality is to be checked by the owner on the private beta.
